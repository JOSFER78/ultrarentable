## 7. ULTRA — EXTREME CONVEXITY RESEARCH

ULTRA is deliberately asymmetric and aggressive. The target is to discover rare, highly convex strategies and campaigns capable of extreme observed growth, including `+1000%+`, without ever forcing that outcome.

The scientific objective is NOT a low-drawdown conventional portfolio. It is to discover positive-skew / fat-tail payoff structures where a bounded sequence of independent attempts can lose small defined risk and occasionally capture very large winners.

The research engine may investigate, when supported by the real venue/instrument policy:

- isolated "bullet" risk units;
- high payoff asymmetry;
- trend expansion / breakout convexity;
- limited, explicitly modeled pyramiding funded by protected open profit;
- dynamic compounding where the research policy permits it;
- harvest / vault mechanisms;
- burst survival across sequences of attempts;
- path-dependent and regime-dependent convexity.

There is NO universal hard-coded leverage, drawdown, risk-per-trade, payout or asset list for ULTRA. Those are execution-policy parameters of each valid instrument/account/venue and must be represented explicitly and evidenced.

`+1000%` is never a gate threshold. A system that produces +1000% by overfit, leakage, unrealistic costs or invalid execution is rejected.

## 8. FONDEO — EXTREME BUT RULE-BOUND FUTURES RESEARCH

`TRACK_FONDEO = FUTURES ONLY`.

No Forex spot/CFD or crypto perpetual enters Fondeo research or certification.

Fondeo has TWO distinct optimization contexts:

```text
EVALUATION
FUNDED / PAYOUT
```

They must never be conflated.

### Evaluation philosophy

The research objective is to maximize the economically meaningful probability of passing quickly while respecting every applicable rule. The system may investigate aggressive but bounded risk policies because the evaluation cost is not the same economic quantity as nominal account size.

Optimize from real policy data for:

```text
P(pass)
expected attempts
expected evaluation cost
expected breach probability
time-to-target
consistency constraints
maximum exposure
```

The system may study short completion windows such as 1–5 trading days, but this is a research horizon, NOT a mandatory assumption. Insufficient opportunity count or regime coverage keeps the result unproven.

### Funded / payout philosophy

After passing, the objective changes to preserving eligibility and extracting durable payouts:

```text
risk-of-ruin
payout survival
maximum drawdown cushion
daily-loss compliance
trailing/static drawdown compliance
position limits
scaling rules
consistency rules
session / close-out rules
```

A policy that is optimal for Evaluation may be invalid or economically irrational for Funded/Payout.

### Policy registry is mandatory

Every Fondeo result must resolve:

```text
firm
product
account_type
account_size
platform
instrument_policy
session_policy
news_policy
overnight_policy
position_limit
max_loss_policy
daily_loss_policy
profit_target
consistency_policy
payout_policy
fee/cost
policy_version
effective_from
effective_to
source_url
source_retrieved_at
source_hash
```

No universal claim such as "4% DD", "1% risk", "50k account", "5 days" or "2 contracts" may be embedded as a global Fondeo truth. Current firm/product rules can change, and old/legacy products can coexist with new ones. ## 9. Discovery / Research integrity

Extreme objectives never relax evidence standards.

The system must account for:

- number of trials;
- genealogy and mutations;
- selection bias;
- blind OOS isolation;
- dataset and engine versions;
- execution-cost realism;
- path dependence;
- multiple-testing / deflated statistics;
- regime coverage;
- strategy-family concentration;
- failed-candidate learning without gate gaming.

A spectacular equity curve is a research observation, not certification.

## 10. Web

The frontend is a renderer of canonical backend evidence.

It must never calculate certification status from cosmetic conditions and must never invent quantitative fallback data.

A repair is complete only when its implementation and evidence are available from the canonical `main` revision and the observable behavior matches the claimed result.
